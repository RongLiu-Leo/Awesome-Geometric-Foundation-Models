Geometric foundation models for 3D and 4D vision

Explore how models recover depth, cameras, pointmaps, and motion from images and video. The papers below are ordered by their first public release.

Papers

  • DUSt3R (2023) — Two image grids become two dense pointmaps expressed in the first camera’s coordinate frame.
  • MASt3R (2024) — A descriptor head adds explicit pixel matching to DUSt3R’s two-view pointmap architecture.
  • VGGT (2025) — Alternating frame and global attention supplies one scene representation to several prediction heads.
  • π³ (2025) — Every view takes the same path through the network, removing the need to pick an anchor.
  • MapAnything (2025) — Optional depth, poses and calibration help one network reconstruct scenes at an estimated metric scale.
  • Depth Anything 3 (2025) — One vision transformer estimates depth and cameras across views, trained with supervision on both depth and rays.
  • VGGT-Ω (2026) — Selected layers pass compact register and camera tokens between views to reduce training memory.
  • Déjà View (2026) — One transformer block revisits the same views, with an adjustable budget for refining geometry.
  • π⁴ (2026) — Two looped decoders reconstruct visible surfaces and track moving points through the recovered 3D scene.
